Privacy Policy — Just Sleep

Last updated 12 September 2026

Just Sleep does not collect, transmit, or store your personal data on any server. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party SDK.

What the app reads

Motion. While an alarm is armed, the Apple Watch app reads wrist motion from the accelerometer during the 30 minutes before your set time. It is used to judge whether you appear to be moving, so the alarm can go off at a lighter moment.

Heart rate (Health data). While an alarm is armed, the watch app reads heart rate samples from Apple Health. This is a secondary signal, and the alarm works without it. Just Sleep never writes anything to Health, and reads no other Health data type.

Where it goes

Nowhere. All of it is processed on your Apple Watch and stays on your devices.

A record of each night — when the alarm fired and why — is written to a private container shared between the Just Sleep apps on your iPhone and Apple Watch, so the "Last night" screen can show it. Only the last seven nights are kept, and older ones are deleted automatically. Deleting the app deletes this record.

Health and motion data are never used for advertising or marketing, never sold, never shared with third parties, and never used for any purpose other than deciding when to wake you.

Subscriptions

Smart wake is an auto-renewing subscription handled entirely by Apple. Purchases are processed by Apple, and Just Sleep never sees your payment details. Whether you are subscribed is determined on your device by Apple's StoreKit, and that answer is not sent anywhere.

Your control

  • Revoke heart rate access at any time in Settings → Health → Data Access & Devices → Just Sleep. The alarm continues to work.
  • Revoke motion access in Settings → Privacy & Security → Motion & Fitness.
  • Deleting the app removes every record it kept.

Children

Just Sleep is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new date.
