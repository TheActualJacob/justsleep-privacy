Common questions

Do I need an Apple Watch?
For the early wake, yes. The watch reads wrist motion, so it has to be worn to bed. Without a watch the app still works as an ordinary alarm on your iPhone.
The watch app did not wake me early.
Open Just Sleep on the watch before bed and tap Arm. watchOS only lets an app schedule its monitoring session while it is open in the foreground, so arming from the iPhone alone is not enough. The alarm still rings at the time you set either way.
Nothing happened at all.
Check that notifications are allowed for Just Sleep, and that the watch was charged and on your wrist. If the watch was on its charger, the iPhone alarm still rings at the set time.
Why did it ring exactly at my alarm time?
Because no clear moment turned up. If you were deeply still for the whole window, or the watch came off, Just Sleep waits for the time you set rather than guessing.
How do I cancel the subscription?
On your iPhone: Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, then Just Sleep. Cancelling leaves you with the ordinary alarm, which is free.
Does it track my sleep?
No. It does not measure sleep stages and does not claim to. It looks for a moment you are already moving in the 30 minutes before your alarm, and wakes you then.
